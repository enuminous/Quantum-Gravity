# QG-025: exploratory protocol after pilot
The pilot compared three rest-frame angles with both outgoing helicities; no thresholds were sealed before that exploratory calculation. The following sweep is specified before its execution. Standard Einstein–Hilbert plus minimally coupled massive real scalar in four dimensions, metric (+---), kappa=1. Evaluate s/u massive exchange, two-graviton scalar contact from the metric expansion, t-channel exchange from the full cubic Einstein–Hilbert Gamma-Gamma expansion with de Donder propagator. Independently compare the complete amplitude against the published scalar-QED factorization and a field-strength contraction. 128 randomized rest-frame scattering events (seed 25001), four helicity pairs each, m in [.5,3], incident omega/m log-uniform [.03,2], angles [.2,pi-.2], random boosts .1–.8 and independently shifted transverse helicity vectors. Test on-shell residual, Lorentz invariance, arbitrary linearized pure-gauge tensor insertion on either external graviton with physical other leg, gauge shifts of both vectors, time reversal (swap incoming/outgoing and conjugate external polarization factors), algebraic factorization, field-strength equality, and rest-frame analytic helicity/cross-section formula. Four leave-one-diagram-out controls compare shifted vs unshifted sums; for each require a detected residual >1e-3 in some cases, not necessarily all. Fixed-angle recoil at omega/m=1e-4 and small-angle behavior 16/theta^4 checked separately. Normalize amplitude errors by max(1,abs(complete reference amplitude)); require <1e-9, mass-shell/momentum checks <1e-10, low-energy relative error <5e-4, forward scaled cross-section error <1e-4 at theta=.005. No loop, UV, emergent gauge, or material claim. Explicit cubic vertex and full sum independently coded from the action, but comparison within same research process; not external replication. Pilot code/log preserved.
